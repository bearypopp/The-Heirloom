Interested in Adopting?

Every cat at The Heirloom is waiting for a special place to call home. Our adoption process is designed to help you find a wonderful match while ensuring each of our treasured cats receives the love, patience, and care they deserve.

What Happens Next?

Once your application is submitted, our team will carefully review your information and reach out using your preferred contact method. We'll help arrange a visit, answer questions and guide you through the next steps towards welcoming your new feline family member.

Things to Keep in Mind

Every senior cat deserves to feel safe, loved, and right at home. Some may settle in quickly, while others need a little extrea time to find their favorite sunny spot or feel comfortable exploring. A gentle routine, cozy spaces, and plenty of love can help your new family member feel like they've always belonged.
